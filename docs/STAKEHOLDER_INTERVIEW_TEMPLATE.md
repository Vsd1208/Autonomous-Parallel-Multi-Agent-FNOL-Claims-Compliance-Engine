# Stakeholder interview template

Questions for a claims adjuster, claims manager, SIU investigator or other insurance professional, to check this
project's assumptions about FNOL against practice. **No interviews have been recorded yet.** The answer sections are
empty on purpose; fill them only with what an interviewee actually said, with their permission.

## Before the interview

- Explain the project in two minutes (the README's first screen) and that it is a student prototype.
- Ask for consent to take notes, and how they want to be credited (name and role, role only, or anonymous).
- Do not ask for, or record, any real claim, customer or company-confidential data.

## Questions

### A. Their work today

1. Walk me through what happens between a customer reporting a loss and your first decision on it.
2. Which part of a new claim takes you the most time? Which part feels least valuable?
3. How do photos arrive today, and how often do you have to ask for more?
4. How do you set the first reserve on a simple auto or property claim?

### B. Deadlines and compliance

5. How do you keep track of acknowledgement and decision deadlines? Have you seen them missed, and why?
6. What does a regulator or auditor ask to see about a claim decision?

### C. Fraud

7. What makes a new claim feel suspicious to you? Which red flags matter most, and which are noise?
8. When do you refer a claim to the SIU, and what information do they need from you?
9. Would a score that says "these details are unusual together" help, or would it add noise? What would make you trust it?

### D. AI assistance

10. Which of this engine's outputs would you use: damage area, estimate, reserve suggestion, deadline dates, fraud
    flags, subrogation hint, the WHY panel? Which would you ignore?
11. What would an AI recommendation need to show before you rely on it?
12. Is "every claim gets a human decision by default" right? For which claims, if any, would you allow
    straight-through processing?

### E. Value

13. If this engine worked as designed, what would change in your week? How would you measure it?
14. What is missing that would make it useful in your team?
